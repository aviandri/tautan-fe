## What looked wrong on screen (not test failures)

These are app observations from the screenshots. No app code was changed.

1. **2 of the 4 starter sections are invisible after onboarding** (shots 05, 07–09). "Pick your starting sections" ticks Shop heading, Testimonials, Email signup and FAQ. The editor then lists all 4 as enabled, but the preview and the public page show only "Shop" and the email signup. Testimonials ("What customers say") and FAQ ("Good to know") are created empty, so they render nothing. A new user ticks 4 sections and sees 2 with no hint why. Suggest seeding 1–2 placeholder items, or showing an "add your first item" state in the editor preview.
2. **New cards go above the "Shop" heading** (shots 12, 14, 20). The product added through "Add card" is inserted at the top of the list, so on the public page it appears above the "Shop" heading that onboarding created to introduce products. That may be intentional (newest first), but it reads wrong for a seller.
3. **Email-signup input is cramped in the phone preview** (shots 06–09). In the onboarding preview and the editor phone preview, the email field is narrower than the Subscribe button, and the placeholder is cut off ("you@exa…"). On the real public page at 390 px (shot 14) it lays out fine.
4. **Insights → Top cards shows "Untitled"** (shot 21) for the cards without a title (store info, media kit). A type-based fallback ("Store info", "Media kit") would read better.
5. **Insights chart:** with one data point, the smoothed line starts below the x-axis and curves up to 1 (shot 21, bottom right). Card taps show 0 after the visitor voted, unlocked a card and subscribed. That's probably correct if those don't count as taps, but worth confirming.
6. **Insights range picker:** the "More" chip shows a tiny stray "▾" dot far to its right (shot 21).

Test artifact, not an app issue: the step banner covers the middle of the onboarding stepper and the dashboard nav in the screenshots.

# Research: liinks.co feature walkthrough

_Researched 2026-11-16. Sources: the public marketing site (home, /features, /pricing, /themes, /tools, /connect, /developers, /compare/linktree), all 84 Liinks help-center articles (most "last updated" Oct 2026), the public OpenAPI 3.1 spec (`/api/v1/openapi.json`), `/llms.txt`, and a logged-in walkthrough of onboarding, the dashboard and the editor on an unsubscribed account (see §0)._

> No liinks.co screenshots are committed. This repo is public, and the screens carry Liinks branding and real customers' pages. Everything is described in prose instead. Nothing was published, no plan or trial was started, and no payment details were entered.

## 0. What was observed first-hand vs from docs

- **First-hand (logged in on an unsubscribed account):**
  - Completed the full onboarding flow (from scratch).
  - Captured every dashboard page.
  - Opened the block picker, three block editors, Header, Socials, Announcement, Footer, Add tab and every Styles group, closing each **without saving**.
  - Nothing was published and no subscription was started. The draft page is visible only to its owner.
- **From Liinks' own docs:** finer detail (edge cases, Pro-only screens, Manage tabs, gates, QR Studio internals). The help articles are very detailed and mostly matched what I saw in the live UI. Places where the live UI differs from the docs are flagged below.

### 0.1 Onboarding, step by step (live)

Admin routes (`/admin/*`) **redirect to `/admin/onboard` until onboarding is complete**. Afterwards they redirect to `/admin/profile/home`. Every step has **Back**, **Skip setup** (top right), a "Next: <step name>" hint, and a **live page preview** on the right that updates as you type.

**Onboarding screen, verbatim** (`/admin/onboard?entry=instagram|website|scratch`; the query param tracks which card is open):

> **How do you want to start?** Pick one. You can change everything later. — top-right **Skip setup**
> - 📸 **Use my Instagram** · _Fastest_ — "Start with your bio, photos and public links. No login needed." → inline field `@ yourhandle`
> - 🔗 **Use my website or link page** — "Bring your links over from Linktree, Beacons, Stan or any website." → inline field `https://linktr.ee/yourname`
> - ✏️ **Start from scratch** — "Tell us what your page is for and we'll set up the first sections." → four intent chips: 📍 **I serve local clients** · 🛍️ **I sell online** · 📣 **I'm building an audience** · ✨ **Something else**
>
> Footer: "Free to build. Your 14-day trial starts when you subscribe to publish." · **Skip this step**

The three options are cards on one screen, and each one expands its own input in place.

**Steps after choosing "Start from scratch → I'm building an audience":** URL `…&persona=audience&step=…`.

1. **`step=profile` — "Introduce yourself"**
   - "Your name, plus an optional photo and short bio."
   - Fields: Profile picture (upload or **Generate with AI**), **Name** (prefilled from the Google account), **Bio**.
   - The preview already shows persona starter content: a marquee "WELCOME TO MY PAGE ✦ …", links "My latest video", "Work with me", "Support my work", and a "Subscribe — Get updates straight to your inbox" email form.
2. **`step=content` — "Choose sections"**
   - "We picked a few sections for growing an audience. Keep them or change the mix."
   - Toggleable section cards: 📢 Scrolling banner, 🔗 Featured links, 📬 Newsletter signup, 🖼️ Photo grid, 🎠 Featured carousel, 🌟 Spotlight card, 👋 About me, ❓ FAQ, 💬 Testimonials, ✉️ Contact form, 📁 Resources folder.
   - The CTA counts the selection: **"Add 3 sections"**. Three were preselected for this persona.
3. **`step=logo` — "We designed your logo"**
   - The AI name-logo designs are already generated by the time you arrive (~5 s).
   - Choices: Block letters, Sticker, Brush script, Art Nouveau, or **Plain text**.
   - **Make it 3D** (badge: New): Flat, Bubblegum, Tangerine, Lime, Blueberry, Chrome, Gold, Holo, Theme, Custom.
   - The CTA changes with the selection: "Use this logo" / "Keep my name as text".
4. **`step=style` — "Choose your look"**
   - "We picked a look for you. Keep it or try another."
   - A grid of all theme cards ("Aa", filled button, outline button). The live list has ~60 names, including several not on the public /themes page: Afterhours, Deep Teal, Riviera, Botanical, Velvet, Midnight Slate, Concrete, Oxblood, Terminal, Espresso, Volt, Moonlight, Ember, Graphite.
   - A "See your logo designs" link goes back to step 3.
5. **`step=slug` — "Claim your URL"** (labelled "Last step")
   - `liinks.co/[your.name]` with a suggestion prefilled from the email local-part (`firstname.lastname`), plus **Finish**.
6. **Completion modal:** "**Meet your Liinks page** — Only you can see this page until you publish it." with a **Go to dashboard** button.

The username is claimed **last**, not first. Auth happens before onboarding, and the slug is the final step.

## 2. Sign-up and onboarding

**Auth methods:**
- **Google** ("Sign up with Google" / "Continue with Google").
- **Email + password.**
- No Apple sign-in and no magic link.

Behaviour:
- A Google identity can be linked or unlinked later under Settings → Google Account (Connect/Disconnect). Unlinking requires a password to be set first.
- Login page: Google button, email + password, "Forgot Password?" (email-based reset at `/i/forgot-password`).
- Email confirmation exists: some features, such as API keys, need a confirmed email.

**Entry points:** the "Sign Up" button, or typing a username into the hero claim bar on the home page.

**Onboarding flow** (observed live; full detail in §0.1):
1. **Starting point:** Instagram handle (public scrape, no IG login), website or link-page URL (Linktree, Beacons, Stan, any site), or from scratch with an intent (local clients / sell online / building an audience / something else). Skip setup is available on every step.
2. **Introduce yourself:** name, photo (or AI-generated), bio.
3. **Choose sections:** persona-based starter sections, preselected.
4. **Your logo:** AI hand-lettered designs + 3D charm, or plain text. This is the page's one free logo set.
5. **Choose your look:** theme grid with one preselected.
6. **Claim your URL:** the slug comes **last**, prefilled from the email. API rules: 3–40 chars, letters, numbers, `.` and `_`, globally unique.
7. "Meet your Liinks page — Only you can see this page until you publish it."

Imported images (profile photo, recent post photos, link thumbnails) land in the Media gallery automatically. Docs also mention an AI "studio portrait" cut-out of your photo during setup. On a brand-new account the Themes group is pinned to the top of the Styles panel for the first day.

**Go-live gate:** until you subscribe, the dashboard shows an **8-step setup checklist** ending in "Publish your page"; after publishing it's replaced by **Page health** (§5). The Publish page button starts the subscription and 14-day trial flow. It wasn't clicked, so whether the trial needs a card wasn't tested.

## 3. Editor

### 3.1 Layout

Admin lives at `/admin/profile/*`; the billing portal is under `/i/admin/*`. The left nav rail has these sections (live; routes in §0.2):
- Search ⌘K, then Home, Edit page
- **Grow:** Analytics, Audience, Forms, AI Chat, QR Codes, Short Links
- **Extras:** Mockups, Print, Referrals
- **Account:** Profiles, Settings
- A "Setup n/8" pill and a persistent **Publish page** button

Inside the editor the rail collapses to icons and expands on hover.

The editor is three columns:
1. **Content (left).** From the top:
   - Tab selector + Add tab / Manage
   - Header (name + Logo button, bio, profile picture)
   - Socials
   - **Announce**
   - Blocks ("Add block" button and a "Paste a link or search…" field)
   - At the bottom: **Deleted blocks** (restore) and **Media gallery**
2. **Live preview (centre).**
   - URL bar on top with share (opens share options incl. QR), copy, and click-to-open-live-page.
   - Hovering the header in the preview opens a quick-edit menu: Display name, Add tab / Manage tabs, …
   - Clicking a block in the preview opens its editor.
3. **Styles panel (right).**
   - Groups: General styles, Block styles, Heading styles, Fonts, Colors, Social & sharing, All styles, Themes.
   - A "Search styles…" box finds any single setting, and **undo/redo arrows** sit beside it.

**Cross-cutting behaviour:**
- **Save model:** opening a block starts one change set. All edits preview live, nothing persists until **Save**, and **Cancel** reverts everything, style overrides included. Closing with unsaved changes prompts to discard. Styles-panel changes apply immediately, with undo/redo.
- **Block editor placement:** the block editor opens beside the list with its own live preview. While a block is open, the Styles panel collapses to a rail and a **Block Styles** panel opens next to the block editor.
- **Command menu (⌘K / Ctrl+K):**
  - Actions: Add Block, Edit Profile Picture, Edit Display Name, Edit Bio, Edit Social Icons, Copy Page Link, View Live Page, Log Out.
  - Navigate: Design, Analytics, Settings, QR Codes, Mockups, AI Chat, Profiles, Billing, Referrals…
  - Deep-links into individual Settings sections ("domain" → Custom Domains, "pixel" → Facebook Pixel).
  - Context-aware: "Subscribe" appears only without a subscription.
- **Mobile editor** (a first-class surface, not a squeezed desktop):
  - The live page fills the screen; the editor is a bottom sheet with a drag handle and 4 snap points (tucked, peek, half, nearly full). It opens at half every time a block opens.
  - Tapping a block in the page opens its editor; taps mean edit, not navigate. Tapping the header or the socials row scrolls to and highlights those sections.
  - With the sheet tucked, a tap restores it without triggering anything.
  - The top bar shows the section name, a menu that slides out the nav, and a search button (the ⌘K equivalent).
- **Help beacon:** a floating button in the bottom corner with a News tab. Achievement-card toasts appear here (§9).

### 3.2 Adding blocks

**Add block picker:**
- A grid of cards, each with an example preview and a hover description.
- Filter chips: **All blocks**, **Popular** (URL button, Image button, Text, Video, Form, Collect emails), **Share resources**, **Showcase my work**, **Tell my story**, **Connect with visitors**, **Build trust**, **Organize my page**.
- **Synonym search:** "podcast" → audio embed, "CV" → Resume, "newsletter" → Collect emails, "coupon" → Discount code, "Calendly" → Booking calendar, "tip" → Donate, "X/Threads" → Follow, "divider/separator" → Heading.
- **Smart paste** into the search box:
  - A media URL opens the embed flow.
  - An email address creates an email button.
  - Any other URL is scraped (OG title, description, image) and pre-fills a link block. If a site blocks the fetch, Liinks retries server-side as `Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; LiinksBot/1.0; +https://www.liinks.co)`.
  - A pasted Calendly link becomes a plain button, not a calendar.
- **New blocks go to the top** of the current tab.

**Block list operations:**
- **Drag to reorder.** Drag onto a folder to nest; drag left to pop out of a folder.
- **Hide/show:** eye icon; hidden blocks show a "Hidden" tag.
- **Delete:** trash icon with a few-seconds **Undo** toast. Older deletes can be restored from **Deleted blocks**, which puts the block back at the bottom of the current tab.
- **Gear / action menu per block:** Schedule, Click Goal, Gate, Skip To Link, Link Analytics.
- **Duplicate:** not mentioned anywhere in the docs. Duplication is documented only at the profile level.
- **Tags:** many blocks have a private **Tag** that names them in the admin list and is searchable there.
- **Admin block search:** works across folders, auto-expanding matches.
- **"Needs attention" badge:** shown on blocks with broken destinations.

### 3.3 Block catalogue and fields

The API discriminator is in brackets. The public write API can create only `link`, `text`, `divider`, `folder` and `media`, but the analytics API returns many more types.

### 3.4 Form builder

Used by Form blocks, Collect-emails blocks **and gates**.
- **Entry points:** Forms panel → **New Form** (full-page builder with live preview), or from a block.
- **Templates:** Contact Form, Newsletter Signup, Waitlist, Event RSVP, Feedback, Q&A / AMA, Booking Inquiry, Lead Capture, Shipping Info, Blank.
- **Settings:**
  - Title, Description
  - Button text (empty → icon button)
  - Success text
  - **Hide question labels** (placeholder-only)
  - **Create new contacts** (auto-on when an Email field exists; forces Email to be required)
  - **Subscribe to emails:** Everyone / Ask with a tick box ("Add me to the mailing list") / No one
- **Field types:** Short text, Long text, Email, Name (first + last), Phone, URL, Number, Dropdown, Multi select, Single select (choices ≥ 2), Date, Time, Date & Time, Address (street/city/region/postal/country; ISO 3166 list with US/CA/GB/AU pinned), Rating (5-star). Each field has a label and a required flag, and fields can be dragged to reorder. At least one field must be required.
- **Responses:**
  - The Forms panel lists forms with response count and last response date.
  - Detail view: preview left, responses right, plus Edit, Copy share URL, **Export CSV**, Delete.
  - Each form shows where it's used, e.g. "1 link gate · 2 tab gates".
  - **Email notification** per submission, formatted as label/value cards.

### 3.5 Gates

"Lock screens", now built on forms. Gates require a paid plan, but can be configured during the trial.
- **Types:** **Password**, **Email** (adds to subscribers), **Confirmation** (age or content warning), **Custom** (any form fields).
- **Attach to:** a link (block gear menu) or a **whole tab** (Manage tabs). Gates are reusable from a "Select Gate" picker.
- Unlocking lasts for the session and applies to every item using the same gate.
- A wrong password shows an inline error. A gated discount code reveals the code instead of opening a link.

### 3.6 Tabs

- Every page starts with a **Home** tab; Home is always pinned first.
- **Add tab:** the button next to the Tab selector, or via the preview header's quick-edit menu.
  - A new tab is created as "New tab"; click its name to rename. Names must be unique.
  - An **"Add a button to <current tab>"** toggle drops a Tab-link button at the bottom of the current tab. It defaults OFF when tab navigation is shown and ON when it isn't.
- **Manage tabs** (shown once there are 2+ tabs; each tab is a card):
  - Drag-reorder; this order drives the menu and pills.
  - Rename.
  - **Tab URL** (Pro): `liinks.co/<user>#shop`. Home can't have one.
  - Copy direct link.
  - **Gate**, Delete.
  - Quick icons on the card plus a ⋯ menu.
- **Per-tab content:** each tab has its own Header, Socials and Blocks. The docs say a new tab can have "blocks, a header, and styles", so styles may be per tab.
- **Linking between tabs:** a Link block with destination = Tab, or a countdown/announcement button pointing at a tab.
- **Visitor view:**
  - Default is a **menu icon** that lists tabs.
  - **Show tab navigation** (General styles; shown once there are 2+ tabs) adds a row of pills at the top. The active tab is highlighted and the row scrolls sideways.
  - Switching is instant, client-side, with no reload.

### 3.7 Announcements

One per page. The **Announce** row sits between Socials and Blocks.
- **Layout:** **Banner** (top bar), **Popup** (fullscreen on arrival), **Card** (bottom corner).
- **Content:** Title, optional description, optional image (popup and card only).
- **Action:** a button opening a link, or a **form**. Popup shows the form inline; banner and card open it.
- **Styling and timing:** custom bg/text colours, plus a **schedule** for start and end.
- **Visitor behaviour:** shown on every visit until dismissed, per device. Clicking the CTA also dismisses it. Editing the announcement doesn't re-show it to people who dismissed it; you have to delete and recreate it.
- **Performance:** views, button clicks, dismissals, view→click conversion, and a trend chart.
- **Preview:** the announcement is hidden in the preview unless its editor is open or the eye toggle is on.

### 3.8 Skip To Link, scheduling, click goals

- **Skip To Link** (block add-on, URL links only):
  - The whole page, custom domain included, redirects to that block's URL.
  - Optional **Schedule Skip End**.
  - Only one block can skip at a time across all tabs.
  - The editor shows a "You are skipping your page — Click here to stop" banner.
  - Each redirect counts as a click on the block and records no page view.
- **Scheduling:** show at a datetime, hide at a datetime, or both for a window. The marketing site also mentions "set days every week".
- **Click goal:** a target number plus a progress modal showing the current count and %.

### 3.9 Media gallery and AI images

- **Media gallery** (bottom of the block list) collects every upload, imported image and AI image.
  - Search by name, rename inline (Enter saves, Esc cancels), delete (doesn't affect blocks already using the image).
  - "Choose from gallery" appears in every image field.
  - **Pro:** the admin can share images to all team profiles.
- **Generate with AI:** available for the profile picture, page background (Image type) and link thumbnails.
  - Prompt plus style: Photo / Illustration / Abstract / Logo / Other. Returns a set of options.
  - **30 images/day.**
- **Header name logo** (AI hand-lettering):
  - "Design my logo" from the name (≤60 chars) takes ~30 s and returns up to 4 designs (script, serif, sans, display) from 15 lettering styles. **One set per page**; a failed set doesn't count.
  - **Upload your own** is the alternative.
  - **Style:** Size, Colors (**Match my text color** or **Keep its own colors**).
  - **Make it 3D:** a WebGL keychain charm that sways and spins on drag. Presets: Bubblegum, Tangerine, Lime, Blueberry, Chrome, Gold, Holo, Theme, Custom. Optional keyring. Customize letters/rim/edge colours, metal, shine, pearl, glitter. Respects reduced-motion, with a flat fallback.
  - The verified badge sits at the logo's top-right.
- **Mockups** (Extras): see §9.

## 4. Design and themes

The Styles panel has a "Search styles…" box and undo/redo.

- Applying one overwrites colours, fonts, background, header layout and block styles, but never content, the logo, or the show-profile-picture state.
- The Social & sharing toggles and Show tab navigation are not part of a theme.
- The selected-theme card un-selects as soon as you tweak any of its styles.

**General styles:**
- **Header layout:**
  - **Classic:** centred avatar, name, bio.
  - **Banner:** page background as a banner, with the avatar on a sheet below.

  - A separate **Desktop background** colour for the area around the centred column on wide screens.
  - Background options are hidden on the Headshot layout.
- **Profile picture:** Show profile picture (not offered on Headshot); size; shadow + style (soft/solid); border + border colour.
- **Picture background**, for see-through avatars: None / Solid / Gradient / Dark / Light.
- **Bio and socials:**
  - **Collapse long bio** (3 lines + "show more")

- **Show tab navigation.**

**Block styles** (page-wide):
- **Tactile blocks:** None / Flat / Concave / Convex / Inset / Glass (frosted).

**Heading styles:** see the Heading block in §3.3.

**Fonts:**
- Two fonts: **Title** (name and headings) and **Text** (bio and body), from the full Google Fonts library (~1,830).

  - Load more.

**Colors** (all in one place): Text colour, Block colour, Block text colour, Label colour + text, Button/CTA colour + text (these appear only once some block uses them), Block border colour. The API adds `headerTextColor` and `sheetColor`.

- **QR code on profile picture:** tapping the avatar shows the QR

- **Verified badge**

## 5. Settings, publishing, domains, profiles

**Settings page:**
- A sidebar of sections with its own **Search settings** filter, matching keywords (e.g. "tracking" → GA and Pixel). Sections are also deep-linkable from ⌘K.
- Sections seen in the docs:
  - **Username:** change the slug. The old URL stops working; there are **no redirects** from old slugs.
  - **Google Account:** connect/disconnect.
  - **Password / email**
  - **UI Settings:** Page Language.
  - **Custom Domains** (Pro)
  - **Google Analytics:** a `G-XXXX` ID with an "Update Google Analytics ID" button. Clear the field to remove.
  - **Facebook Pixel:** a Pixel ID.
  - **Pinterest verification**
  - **Contacts / Contact Collection Settings:** Storage = Mailchimp / Klaviyo / Flodesk / Kit, an API key validated ~2 s after typing, then a destination audience/list/segment/form.
  - **Connect Instagram**
  - **Request verification:** needs a linked IG/X/TikTok icon whose profile links back; reviewed within 72 h.
  - **API Keys:** create (shown once, hashed) and revoke. OAuth-connected assistants also appear here.
  - **Customize sharing metadata** (Pro): title, description, image for social previews.
  - **Email Preferences** (opened from email links): opt out of the weekly digest / marketing / all non-essential email.

**Go live / publish:**
- There is **no explicit publish toggle**. A page is live if and only if the account has an active subscription or trial.
- "Unpublishing" happens by cancelling the subscription: the page stays live until the period ends.
- **Hide** works per block. Skip To Link turns the whole page into a redirect.

**Share preview / SEO:**

**Custom domain flow** (Pro):
1. Settings → Custom Domains → **Connect Existing Domain**, then enter a domain. A root domain or a subdomain both work.
2. A root domain creates **two entries** (apex + `www`); a subdomain creates one.
3. For each entry, **Connect** shows the DNS values with Copy buttons:
   - an **A record** → Liinks IP, and
   - a **TXT record** `liinks_<token>` for verification.
4. Press **Connect** again; a spinner shows while Liinks verifies, then a **Connected** tag appears.
5. Domains failing to connect surface in Page health.
6. **Team-level domains:** the admin picks which managed profile a domain points at.

**QR codes (QR Studio):**

  - Pattern: Square / Dots / Rounded / Fluid / Diamond

- **Scannability meter:** Scannable / With warnings / Will not scan, with one-click fixes. A red meter blocks export.
- **Export:**
  - PNG at 1024/2048/4096 px, or SVG.
  - Liinks decodes the rendered file before download to verify it.
  - The filename comes from the code name.
- **Analytics** (dynamic codes): scans over time, plus breakdowns by country and device.

**Short links:**
- `liinks.cc/<auto code>`, paid plans only.
- Destination URL plus a private Label.

**Multiple profiles / Teams** (Pro):

## 8. Developers, API and AI assistants

**REST API** (beta but versioned, `/api/v1`):
- Open to **every account with a confirmed email**, no subscription needed.
- **Auth:** `Authorization: Bearer liinks_live_…` from Admin → Settings → API Keys. Keys are shown once, hashed at rest, revocable, and unscoped.
- No sandbox: you test on an un-subscribed (non-live) profile.
- **Endpoints:**
  - `GET /me`
  - `GET|POST /profiles`
  - `GET|PATCH|DELETE /profiles/{id}`
  - `GET|PATCH /profiles/{id}/styles`
  - `GET|PUT /profiles/{id}/socials` (replace the whole ordered row)
  - `GET|POST /profiles/{id}/blocks`
  - `GET|PATCH|DELETE /profiles/{id}/blocks/{blockId}`
  - `POST /profiles/{id}/blocks/reorder`
  - `GET /profiles/{id}/pages` (tabs)
  - `GET /profiles/{id}/form-submissions`
  - `GET /profiles/{id}/subscribers`
  - `GET /profiles/{id}/analytics`
  - `GET /profiles/{id}/analytics/breakdown`
  - `POST /profiles/{id}/screenshot`
- **Profile fields:** `slug` (3–40), `displayName`, `bio` (≤500, plain text), `tagline` (≤120), `profilePictureUrl` (re-hosted by the server).
- **Styles fields** (a good reference model for our theme overrides):
  - **Colours:** `buttonColor`, `buttonTextColor`, `labelColor`, `labelTextColor`, `ctaColor`, `ctaTextColor`, `foregroundColor`, `sheetColor`, `headerTextColor`, `borderColor`.
  - **Header:** `headerLayout` (CLASSIC | BANNER | BUSINESS | HEADSHOT), `enableSheetFade`, `sheetEdge` (STRAIGHT | ROUNDED | CURVED).
  - **Fonts:** `fontStyle1`, `fontStyle2` (Google font names).
  - **Background:** `background` {`backgroundType`: SOLID | GRADIENT | SPLIT | IMAGE | ANIMATED | NONE, `backgroundValue`: CSS colour or gradient string}, `desktopBackground` (SOLID | NONE), `backgroundImageUrl`.

- **Block create** is a discriminated union on `type`:
  - `link` {title, url, layout, caption, thumbnailUrl, labelText, couponCode, gridSize 2|3, textAlign, emoji, hidden, pageId (default `home`), folderId}

  - `divider` {title, textAlign}
  - `folder` {title, style: collapsible|inline}
  - `media` {url, title, embedOptions}
- **Errors** use one envelope, `{error:{code,message,details}}`. Codes: validation_error 400 (per-field issues tree), unauthorized 401, **plan_required 402**, forbidden 403, not_found 404, conflict 409, rate_limited 429, server_error 500.
- **Rate limits:** 120 req/min and 30 writes/min per key, rolling 60 s window. `RateLimit-Policy`, `RateLimit-Limit`, `RateLimit-Remaining` and `RateLimit-Reset` headers; `Retry-After` on 429.
- **Versioning:** in the URL; additive within a major version; `Deprecation`/`Sunset` headers ≥180 days ahead of removal.
- **Machine-readable resources:**
  - Interactive docs `/api/docs`
  - OpenAPI `/api/openapi.json`
  - Discovery root `/api`
  - `/llms.txt`
  - "Agent skills"

**MCP server** (`https://www.liinks.co/api/mcp`, Streamable HTTP):

## Gaps vs SPEC

The baseline is the MVP summary in the request plus `src/lib/types.ts`:
- email auth
- 10 block types: link, text, image, video, music, social_icons, folder, faq, testimonial, contact_form
- tabs
- 8 themes + overrides (`bg` color/gradient/image, `button` fill/outline/shadow + radius + colours, one `font`, `text_color`)
- `is_published` toggle
- SEO title/desc/image
- analytics (views/clicks/CTR/top links/referrers)
- QR code
- contact inbox

Each tier below is ordered by how often the feature shows up in Liinks' own marketing and docs.

### A. Differences in existing SPEC features (worth deciding early, cheap now)

1. **Publishing model.** Liinks has no publish toggle: live = subscribed. Our `is_published` toggle is fine for an MVP without billing, but note that Liinks pages are "free to build, pay to go live".
2. **Video and music are one "media" block** in Liinks (Iframely, any oEmbed URL), with Audio and Video only as two picker entries. Consider merging `video` + `music` into one `media {url, embed_options}` type.
3. **The image block is a link destination** in Liinks (Link → Image, with Full bleed / Disable click and a lightbox), not a separate type. Our `image.link_url` is fine; add a **lightbox** and **full-bleed** option.
4. **Link block is much richer.** Missing fields:
   - `description`/caption
   - `layout` (button | thumbnail | thumbnail_highlight | grid 2/3 | image_background | carousel)
   - icon/emoji/3D-icon imagery
   - `label` badge
   - `text_align`
   - destination kinds email / file / tab
5. **Folder modes:** Liinks has `collapsible` vs `inline` (admin-only group) and **nested folders**. Our `parent_id` supports nesting, but `FolderData` lacks `style`.
6. **Contact form → general form builder.** Liinks forms have typed fields (16 types), templates, required flags, success text, hidden labels, standalone form URLs, CSV export, and email notifications. Our `contact_form {title, button_label}` is a fixed form.
7. **Testimonial items** lack `role/company`, `url`, and a heading title/description; **FAQ** lacks a heading title/description. Liinks FAQ is single-open.
8. **Theme model is too narrow** next to Liinks styles:
   - Missing: **header layout** (classic/banner/business/headshot + sheet colour/edge/fade), **two fonts** (title + text), separate **block vs button vs label colours**, **border width + colour**, **shadow** (amount + soft/solid), **spacing**, **tactile** surfaces, **split/animated** backgrounds, and a **desktop background**.
   - Liinks stores these as ratios in [0, 1]. See §8 for a ready-made field list.
9. **Per-block style overrides** (chain-link inherit/override) aren't in SPEC.
10. **Analytics** is missing:
    - unique visitors and engagement
    - **period-over-period %**
    - time-range presets + custom range
    - hourly/daily/weekly/monthly buckets
    - separate social clicks
    - breakdowns by **country, device, browser, OS, UTM**, plus traffic channel
    - **click-through filtering**
    - per-link chart
    - CSV export
11. **SEO / share preview:** Liinks makes custom OG Pro-only and auto-generates it otherwise. We have it in MVP, which is fine.
12. **Auth:** Liinks offers **Google sign-in** alongside email (no Apple) and lets you link or unlink Google later. Password reset is email-based.
13. **Username rules:** Liinks allows 3–40 chars with mixed case accepted; ours is lowercase 3–30. Changing a username **breaks the old URL** on Liinks (no redirect). Decide whether we redirect.
14. **Delete:** Liinks has an undo toast plus a "Deleted blocks" restore list (soft delete). New blocks are inserted at the **top**.
